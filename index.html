Model Park

AI models as a city, all at one scale. Each company is a block, each model is a building built from its real architecture: floors are layers, floor area is parameters, lit cells are the experts working on a token.

Solid buildings come from published configs. Dashed silhouettes are closed models: their size is not public, so it is our estimate.

Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, click or tap a building for its card.
